About this Edition

This synopsis integrates six corpora relevant to the study of Tatian's Diatessaron and the Old Syriac Gospels, presented as a unified digital edition for the purpose of computational textual analysis.

Corpora

  • Greek New Testament — SBL Greek New Testament (SBLGNT), via MorphGNT.
  • Peshitta New Testament — ETCBC's Text-Fabric dataset of the Syriac New Testament.
  • Old Syriac Gospels — Curetonian (SyrC) and Sinaiticus (SyrS) transcribed by the Comprehensive Aramaic Lexicon (cal.huc.edu), retrieved in both CAL romanization and Syriac Unicode.
  • Diatessaron (Arabic) — Ciasca's 1888 edition of the Arabic recension attributed to Abū 'l-Faraj ʿAbdallāh Ibn al-Ṭayyib, digitized via a three-way OCR pipeline (QARI-OCR, Tesseract, Kraken) with multimodal correction.
  • Diatessaron (English) — Hope W. Hogg's translation in Ante-Nicene Fathers, vol. IX, scraped from Wikisource.
  • Source mappings — Hill's 1894 verse-level mapping of the Diatessaron to the four canonical gospels (2,408 entries) and Ciasca's own Ordo Diatessari in versione Arabica (955 entries).

Scope & caveats

This edition is a research scaffold, not a critical edition. OCR errors persist in the Arabic text (particularly around decorative borders); section markers were not always recoverable (sections 5–8, 11, 25, 30, 34–35, 46, 53 are absorbed into adjacent sections). The Old Syriac coverage reflects extant manuscript witnesses — Curetonian lacks Mark almost entirely and has large gaps elsewhere.

Method

The Arabic text was produced through a pipeline combining:

  1. PDF page extraction at 300 DPI.
  2. Three parallel OCR passes: QARI-OCR (a Qwen2-VL-based vision-language model fine-tuned for Arabic, running on a remote GPU), Tesseract 5 with the ara_best model, and Kraken with OpenITI's classical Arabic print models.
  3. Page-level multimodal correction: each of ~210 pages was cross-referenced against the original image and the three OCR candidates to produce a consolidated Arabic text.
  4. Section segmentation using pattern matching on الاصحاح markers with a longest-increasing-subsequence constraint to reject spurious matches.
